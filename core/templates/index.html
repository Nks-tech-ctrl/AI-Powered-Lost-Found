Safe & Responsible Reunions

Built for safe and responsible reunions.

We designed FindBack from the ground up to protect personal privacy, deter fraudulent claims, and ensure safe exchanges between community members.

Privacy-Conscious Reporting

Sensitive serial codes, phone numbers, and full names are masked. Public cards never expose personal identifying data.

Claim Verification

Claimants must answer hidden verification questions (wallpaper, internal engravings, receipts) before handovers occur.

Secure Proxy Messaging

Communicate through an encrypted internal messaging proxy without sharing personal phone numbers or home addresses.

Safe Handover Hubs

Designated partner safe zones—including police lobbies, transit customer desks, and campus libraries—for secure meetups.

Our Responsible Platform Pledge

FindBack is designed to support honest community recovery without monetary ransom or hostage incentives.
